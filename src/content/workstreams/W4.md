---
id: W4
title: "Continual learning: metaplastic soft consolidation"
tagline: Can slowing learning on important weights replace freezing them?
---

Hard freezing never forgets, but it spends network capacity quickly, and frozen weights can never help later tasks. Our second approach borrows the idea of *metaplasticity* from neuroscience. Instead of freezing important weights, we lower their learning rate in proportion to how important they were, so they can still change a little.

The approach also sizes each task's subnetwork adaptively. It keeps units until a set share of the importance is covered, rather than a fixed percentage, so that easy tasks take less room than hard ones. We will measure whether this keeps accuracy while using less capacity, and how much forgetting it allows in exchange.

This work is planned for late December 2026 to January 2027.
