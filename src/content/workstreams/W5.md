tagline: How does our framework compare, and what is the final story?

In February and March 2027 we bring everything together:
- **Final runs.** We compare our approaches with established continual learning methods (PackNet, Winning SubNetworks and Elastic Weight Consolidation) on Split-MNIST, Split-CIFAR-10 and Split-CIFAR-100. Every configuration is frozen first, and we look at the test set only once.
- **What we report.** For each method we give average accuracy, the share of the network each task uses, how much retraining each task needs, and how much earlier tasks help later ones. Each number comes with its spread over five seeds.
- **The thesis.** The lab notes on this site are written as drafts of its chapters, so the thesis grows from the record of what we actually did.
